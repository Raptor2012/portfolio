Game · weekend build · solo

Do.Die

A personal weekend jam, inspired by Max Payne and Control, built by directing a coding agent and released as a Unity WebGL build.

Telemetry logs deaths on a heat map, damage by enemy type, weapon and ability use, and progress. About 30 people played it on itch.io and 5 finished a run, which showed where most players died and which abilities they never touched.
